Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We take great care in preparing and packaging every VALORA LUXURY order. Here's what to expect.

Processing Time

Orders are processed and dispatched within 2–4 business days of confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will begin processing on the next business day.

Delivery Timelines

Once dispatched, orders typically arrive within 5–9 business days, depending on your location within India. Delivery timelines may vary during peak seasons or due to circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Charges

We offer complimentary shipping on all orders above ₹15,000. A flat shipping fee applies to orders below this amount, calculated and displayed at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation with tracking details so you can follow your delivery's progress.

International Shipping

At this time, VALORA LUXURY ships within India only. International shipping may be introduced in the future.

Delivery Issues

If your order has not arrived within the expected timeframe, or if you notice any issue upon delivery, please contact our team via the Contact page and we will investigate promptly.

Contact Us

For any shipping-related questions, reach out via our Contact page.
