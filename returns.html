Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At VALORA LUXURY, we want you to love every piece you bring home. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

Return Window

Most items may be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as Final Sale
  • Earrings and other pierced accessories, for hygiene reasons
  • Items showing signs of wear or use
  • Items without original packaging or tags

How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please contact our team via the Contact page with your order number and reason for return. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed to your original method of payment within 7–10 business days. Please note that our online payment functionality is not yet live; refund timelines will be finalised once payments are enabled.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size or colour, please indicate this when initiating your return, subject to availability.

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer, unless the item received was damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item, and we will arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost to you.

Contact Us

For any questions about returns or refunds, please reach out via our Contact page.
